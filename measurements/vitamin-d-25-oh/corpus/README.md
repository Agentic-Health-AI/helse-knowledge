# Corpus

Discovery, relations and screening are separate canonical JSONL records. The committed records use
only `example.invalid` identifiers and synthetic aggregate placeholders to prove state and relation
checks. Real, unscreened PubMed metadata is stored in the
[2026-08-29 discovery collection](../collection/2026-08-29/README.md). Discovery cannot feed a
medical synthesis until screening and source-bound extraction are complete.
